Frequently Asked Questions

Which Sadhna.co incense sticks are best for daily meditation and how long do they burn?

Our Oudh and Sandalwood Bambooless Incense Sticks burn for 35–40 minutes, offering a steady, natural aroma ideal for daily meditation and evening pooja.

What makes Sadhna.co’s dhoop cones eco-friendly and low smoke?

We use 100% charcoal-free resins and sustainably sourced herbs, resulting in richly fragrant Dhoop Cones that produce minimal smoke and no harmful emissions.

How do I choose the right Havan Cup for Ganesh Puja or homa ceremonies?

Our Organic Havan Cups come in different sizes and fragrances. For Ganesh Puja, choose our Sandalwood Havan Cup for its calming scent; for homa rituals, the Camphor refill pack gives a purifying smoke.

Are Sadhna.co attar sprays suitable as natural home fragrances and aromatherapy?

Yes—our Attar Sprays blend essential oils and traditional herbs, providing uplifts for mood and focus while doubling as safe, chemical-free home fragrances.

Can I use Sadhna.co products as a sustainable gift for spiritual practitioners?

Absolutely—our curated Gift Bundles and Refill Packs are eco-luxury presents, packaged in recyclable materials, perfect for devotees and wellness enthusiasts.

Do you offer nationwide shipping and how soon will my Sadhna.co order arrive?

We ship pan-India with free delivery on Prepaid orders. Orders typically dispatch within 1–2 business days and arrive within 3–5 days.

How does Sadhna.co support Gau Mata protection and social impact initiatives?

A portion of every sale funds cow protection campaigns at Delhi NCR and Haryana gaushalas. To date, we’ve supported over 200 cows through our structured program.

What is your return policy for pooja samagri and how do I request a replacement?

Due to the sacred nature of our products, we offer replacements for any damaged items. Simply email support@sadhna.co within 24 hrs with your order details and photos.
